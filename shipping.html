Shipping and Delivery Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

1. Online courses (no shipping)

  • Online courses are delivered digitally. Nothing is posted to you.
  • After your payment is successful, we send the joining details (class link, group link or login) to your email or WhatsApp within 24 hours.
  • If you have not received them within 24 hours, please contact us.

2. Books

  • Where we ship: within India only.
  • Dispatch: within 2 to 3 working days after payment.
  • Delivery: usually 5 to 10 working days after dispatch, by India Post or a courier service, depending on your location.
  • Delivery charges: shown clearly before you pay.
  • Tracking: we share the tracking number by WhatsApp or email once your order is dispatched.

3. Address and delays

  • Please check that your delivery address and phone number are correct. We are not responsible for delays caused by an incorrect address.
  • Delivery may take longer in remote areas, during festivals or because of events outside our control.
  • If your book arrives damaged, please see our Refund and Cancellation Policy.
